Angles are aerospace Z-Y-X yaw, pitch and roll of the IMU body (forward/right/down). Sensor mounting is the rotation part of the calibration's correction matrix C (C = R S, S symmetric): the rotation from the sensor's axes to the IMU body that the calibration applies. The IMU method fits it; the ellipsoid method takes it from the declared Mounting option. Declination is the fitted Earth field's angle east of north, and inclination its dip below horizontal (IMU method only). The calibration scan's yaw/pitch/roll are stored in calibration files fitted in the Calibration tab from scans with attitude. Scan angles are plotted over the sample index from the file's first row.
