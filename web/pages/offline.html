Open a magnetic and/or electromagnetic file.

Values are in nT, converted from the unit in the column name (T, µT, nT, gauss; no unit means nT). The magnetic vector is combined_field_T (Earth plus anomaly) when present, else columns such as Bx/By/Bz or the VN-100 recording's magnetic_x_gauss…. The electromagnetic phasor is field_T.real/imag or Bx_re/Bx_im-style columns; its amplitude is the peak √(Re² + Im²) and its phase atan2(Im, Re). Axes are E/N/U for east-north-up files, else x/y/z. θ is the angle of the third axis from the file's third axis, φ its azimuth from the first axis; rotated axes are primed and |B| is the same in every frame. Calibration crops and calibrated CSVs open here too. Apply Calibration corrects the open magnetic file with the calibration JSON, C (Braw − b), and shows B east/north/up in nT (rows with a missing B or angle are dropped); the scan's yaw/pitch/roll rotate it to east/north/up when present, else the sensor is taken as level and facing north. It then opens a window with the calibration's angles (sensor mounting, Earth field, and the calibration scan's yaw/pitch/roll when the file holds them) and the yaw/pitch/roll of the open scans.
