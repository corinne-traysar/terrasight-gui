Rotate slowly while keeping the sensor’s center in the same place, away from steel, magnets and power supplies. X, Y and Z measure the field along the sensor’s own axes. Their values should change with orientation; total field √(X² + Y² + Z²) should stay approximately constant in a uniform field. Nearby objects and changes in position can change the total.

9600 baud · times are Pi receipt times · sensor axes follow the package markings · measurement accuracy and timing alignment with other sensors remain unverified.
